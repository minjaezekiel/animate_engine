TITLE: The Keeper

## SCENE: the harbour at dusk
[The sea works against the black rocks. A lighthouse stands above them.]

MARA
My grandmother lit this lamp for forty years. My mother for thirty. The light has never failed.

MARA (quietly)
Tonight it failed.

## SCENE: the long climb
[Two hundred and eleven steps. Mara climbs. Jonas follows.]

JONAS
You should not have come out here alone.

MARA
Someone had to. The boats are still out there, Jonas. Four of them.

JONAS
Then we fix it together, or we do not fix it at all.

## SCENE: the lamp room
[Brass and broken glass. The great lens sits dark.]

MARA
The mechanism is seized. The oil has gone to tar in the cold.

JONAS
We have no replacement. Not before morning.

MARA
Then we do it the old way. We turn it by hand.

## SCENE: the light returns
[Slowly, the beam swings out across the water.]

JONAS
How long can we keep this up?

MARA
Until the last boat is home.
