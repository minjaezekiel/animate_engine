# Completed phases

Records of work that is **done**. Each one is the account of a film or a series
that was actually finished, and of the engine defects building it exposed —
because that is how every feature in this engine arrived: an animation hit a
limit, and the phase shipped the feature that removed it.

Open work lives in [../pending/](../pending/). The one-paragraph-per-phase
register, newest first, is [../STATUS.md](../STATUS.md); it covers every phase
including the ones with no document of their own.
