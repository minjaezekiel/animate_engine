# Madini ya Vito — titles and captions for posting

Six videos, 1080×1920 (9:16), MP4 (H.264 + AAC). Plays natively on TikTok,
Instagram Reels, Facebook Reels, YouTube Shorts and WhatsApp Status; the
Facebook and WhatsApp feeds take it unchanged.

Each part has **three title options**. They are different shapes on purpose —
the retention sources agree that hooks that hold are one of a small number of
forms, and which one works for an audience is something only the numbers tell
you. Post a different shape on each platform for the same video and compare.

- **A — the number.** A specific, checkable fact.
- **B — the open loop.** A question the video answers and the title does not.
- **C — the direct address.** Speaks to the viewer's own situation.

Keep the title under about 12 words. The first words matter most: on most
feeds the rest is truncated.

## Posting notes

**Order.** Parts 3 and 5 are the most immediately useful to a general
audience and the most likely to be shared — they tell someone what to do
rather than what is true. If the series is not doing well from part 1, post
part 3 as a standalone and link the rest from it.

**Sound off.** Every line is burned in as a subtitle, so each video works
muted. Do not add a platform caption that covers the lower third — the
subtitles sit at 1548 of 1920 and platform UI sits below that.

**The first three seconds carry no title card** on purpose: the narration
starts at 0.3 s and the title appears underneath it a few seconds in. Do not
trim the opening.

**Thumbnails.** Part 1's gem at about 0:04 and part 3's river at about 0:40
are the strongest single frames in the series.

**Measure.** The reason there are three title shapes is that nobody — this
included — knows which works for this audience. Use the same video with a
different title per platform and compare the 3-second retention, not the
view count.
